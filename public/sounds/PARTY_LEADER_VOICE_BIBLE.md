# Party Leader Voice Bible

## Shared direction

- Language: English.
- Performance: premium animated fantasy animal. The voice sits between a human actor and a talking cartoon animal: fully intelligible, but clearly non-human in resonance, rhythm, and little reactions.
- Animal balance for the remaining leaders: roughly 60% talking-cartoon-animal character and 40% human clarity. Species should be immediately audible through vocal placement, reactions, rhythm, and comic timing, without adding a literal animal noise to every line.
- Locked pilot exception: The Shadow Claw keeps the approved Voice 1 performance, which is subtler and more human than the new 60/40 direction.
- Length: usually 0.8-2.2 seconds; one clean thought per clip.
- Mix: close microphone, dry studio sound, no music, no reverb tail, no creature effects baked into the voice.
- Avoid: ordinary realistic human voices, mascot squeakiness, baby voices, trailer voice, mobile-game shouting, parody accents, exaggerated whispers, and repeated animal noises.
- Use ElevenLabs Voice Design for a distinct base voice, then the most expressive speech model for the final performance.
